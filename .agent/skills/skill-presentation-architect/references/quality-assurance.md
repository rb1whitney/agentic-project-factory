# Quality Assurance

## Narrative QA

- Big Idea is one sentence.
- Point of view and stakes are explicit.
- Audience persona is plausible.
- No more than three primary takeaways.
- Narrative works without slides.
- Story structure fits the material.
- Story transitions are causal.
- Ending returns to stakes and action.

## Storyboard QA

- Titles are assertions.
- Titles alone tell the story.
- Each slide has one purpose.
- Each slide has a visual thesis.
- No redundant slides.
- Objections appear where needed.
- Ask is explicit.

## Evidence QA

- Every material number has a status.
- Derived numbers show arithmetic.
- Unknowns are bracketed.
- Conflicts are surfaced.
- Technical claims are traceable.

## Visual QA

- One dominant idea per slide.
- No clipping.
- No overflow.
- No illegible text.
- Strong hierarchy.
- Consistent alignment.
- Diagrams are understandable at presentation distance.
- Color semantics are consistent.
- No decorative filler.

## PowerPoint QA

- Text editable.
- Shapes editable where practical.
- Charts editable where practical.
- Speaker notes present.
- Fonts available / embedded or substituted intentionally.
- Slide dimensions correct.
- No accidental off-canvas objects.
- Rendered deck inspected.
- Thumbnail sequence tells a coherent story.

## Final decision QA

Ask:
- What decision is being requested?
- Why now?
- Why this recommendation?
- What alternatives were rejected?
- What is the biggest risk?
- Who owns the next step?
