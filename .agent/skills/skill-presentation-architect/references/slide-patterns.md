# Slide Pattern Library

## 1. COVER
Purpose: establish Big Idea and stakes.

Composition:
- 60/40 split;
- eyebrow;
- assertion title;
- concise subhead;
- explicit ask;
- visual preview of the transition.

## 2. COST EQUATION
Purpose: make economics auditable.

Composition:
- operand cards;
- × and = operators;
- annual cost of inaction;
- cost to fix;
- payback.

Do not use until material inputs are sourced or explicitly bracketed.

## 3. ASYMMETRIC ARCHITECTURE FLOW
Purpose: explain a system relationship.

Composition:
- executive BLUF;
- three technical pillars;
- dominant architecture visual.

## 4. BEFORE / AFTER
Purpose: make a transformation concrete.

Show:
- current state;
- friction;
- target state;
- guarantees / boundaries.

## 5. FAILURE & VALIDATION GATE
Purpose: expose risk and control.

Show:
- failure point;
- consequence;
- declarative gate;
- verification signal.

## 6. PHASED ROADMAP & DECISION
Purpose: convert recommendation into executable decision.

Show:
- three phases;
- gates;
- ownership;
- alternatives;
- recommendation.

## 7. FULL-CANVAS CLAIM
Purpose: when the idea itself deserves the entire frame.

Use one dominant statement with minimal supporting evidence.

## 8. COMPARISON / TRADEOFF
Purpose: make alternatives explicit.

Use aligned criteria, not marketing-style winner highlighting.

## 9. HERO METRIC
Purpose: one decisive quantitative fact.

Use when the number is materially more powerful than a chart.

## 10. DIAGRAM SPINE
Purpose: a core diagram is the narrative's organizing model.

Use when the architecture / causal model is itself the key insight.

Pattern selection is subordinate to the claim.
